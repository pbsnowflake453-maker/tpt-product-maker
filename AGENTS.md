# AGENTS.md — rules for any AI agent editing TpT Product Maker

**Ask Samantha before publishing changes.** Never deploy, buy, or post on her behalf without her explicit OK. Batch changes: every re-deploy costs effort, so gather edits, test locally, and publish together when she says so. She likes to talk through ideas before anything is built ("still just talking" = don't build yet).

## Owner & brand
- Store: **My Side of the Classroom** (Teachers Pay Teachers). Logo: `site/assets/logo.png` (apple "TEACH ♥ LOVE ♥ INSPIRE").
- Every product: cover, directions/how-to page, product pages, Terms of Use page (store's standard terms text + logo), footer "© My Side of the Classroom".
- **Black and white only**, except the logo and the Mystery Picture color key / colored answer key. **No clipart. No grade levels anywhere.**
- **Name line at the very top** of every student page.
- Skill shown in a rounded **"Skill: …" bubble** (Andika Bold) — top right on boards/sheets. Bundles: "Skills: a • b • c".

## Fonts (Google Fonts only)
- Titles: **Pacifico** (e.g. "Roll and Read", "Answer Key", "How to Play").
- Body and skill bubble: **Andika** (Regular/Bold). Sniglet was rejected as "too bubbly" for the skill bubble.
- Bingo title only: **Fredoka Bold**, big, bubble-letter style.
- Fonts are embedded in the PDF (jsPDF) and referenced by name in the PPTX.

## Output formats
- **PowerPoint is the main download** (editable in Google Slides without signing in); PDF also offered.
- **Google Slides safety:** no theme colors, no table styles (draw grids with shapes/lines, not tables), explicit white fills / white slide background, Google Fonts only. A previous version showed blue shading in Slides because of table styles — never reintroduce them.
- Samantha edits odd words herself in the PPTX; don't add explanations of why words were chosen or "fewer than 36 words" warnings to the documents.

## Reading skills & words (Mega Word Bank)
- **Only source of reading words: Samantha's Mega Word Bank** (`data-src/phonics_words.csv`, G1 Units 1-7 of the Amplify CKLA 3rd ed. scope & sequence). The old audited list, K, G2 and Dolch/Fry are gone. Regenerate with `python3 spec/build_from_megabank.py` (writes `site/data/skills.js` + `data-src/bank_index.json`). Never add words by hand to the generated files.
- Default pull: `form=base`, `tricky=no`, `is_name=no`. Decodable for a skill = its pool words with unit end <= the skill's placement; words from later units are **Challenge** words. Cutoffs are **unit level** (the bank has per-word lesson numbers, but skills use the end of the unit), so Mystery Picture background pools and Word Maze `pos` filters are unit level too.
- Options in the picker: **Unit 1-7** (words new in that unit), **Through Unit 1-7** (cumulative), **Categories** (pooled groups: Short Vowel CVC, FLOSS, blends, digraphs, Magic e, long vowel teams, r-controlled incl. "ar, or, er", etc.), and **Single Skills**. No "G1" in names. No unit options outside 1-7.
- **Display names:** K/G1 skills have no example words in names (menus, bubbles, covers, dividers, keys). Exception: **oo (look)**, **oo (soon)**. Minimum disambiguators where dropping would collide: th (thin)/th (this), -s (/s/)/-s (/z/)/s (/z/ inside a word), -ed (/t/)/(/d/)/(/ed/); blends are "CVCC ending blends", "CCVC beginning blends", "3-letter blends". Change labels only (`disp` field), never internal ids or word data. Bubble style is "/ou/", "short a", etc. The skill "s (/z/ inside a word)" is tiny (his, has, as).
- **Decodable sentences** (`data-src/sentences/u1..u7.txt`, checked and compiled by `python3 spec/build_sentences.py` into `site/data/sentences.js`): every word must be a bank word (any form) from that unit or earlier, a unit-introduced tricky word, or an approved character name (Beth, Nat | Gran, Josh, Jen | Vern | Kate, Max | Grace, Jill, Pepper | Kay, Martez; earlier names may appear later; Unit 3 uses fable animals as plain common nouns, no new names). Each sentence needs at least one word new in its unit. The script fails on any violation; keep it at 0.

## Math skills (js/math.js)
Add/subtract/mixed within 5, 10, 20; 10 more/10 less; tens ± tens (40+20); two-digit ± tens (43±20); two-digit ± two-digit no regrouping (43±25); two-digit addition with regrouping within 100 (43+28); addition facts +0..+10; subtraction facts −0..−10; multiplication by factor ×0..×12; mixed single-digit multiplication; division by divisor; doubles; near doubles; making 10; missing addends; ×10 and ×100. Every math product has an **answer key**.

## Product rules
- **Roll and Read** (landscape): 6x6 grid under die faces, 3 boards with different mixes (no difficulty progression). The 3rd board is a **"Challenge!"** board only if there are ≥12 challenge words (challenge is relative to the skill's own syllable baseline: a 2-syllable list's challenge is 3+ syllables); otherwise a regular board. Word List page split Decodable / Challenge and lists **only the words actually used on the boards**. Large pools are sampled randomly.
- **Roll and Solve** (landscape): same board layout with problems; **3 boards, no Challenge board**, an answer key per board (answer bold + underlined). Repeats never in the same row/column.
- **Task Cards** (landscape): **24 cards A–X, 4 per page**, cute double border (dashed "stitched" inner border, tiny hearts in corners), letter circle, **small** skill bubble, **no directions on the cards**, **thick dotted cut lines**. "How to Use" page with **bold way-to-use titles** (scoot, center, pass it on, write the room, quiz-quiz-trade, small group, early finishers, grab and go, self-check, tip). **3 recording sheets**: blanks beside letters in 2 columns; boxes with letters (6x4, boxes touching); bigger boxes across 2 pages (4x3 each, touching). Answer key page. Math only for now.
- **Bingo** (portrait, one board per page so she can print 2 per page): reading or math; she picks number of boards and size 3x3/4x4/5x5. **FREE space only on 5x5** (star + "FREE" regular weight, all caps); **3x3 and 4x4 have no free space**. Default **same words/answers on every board** (1st graders get upset if a called word isn't on their card) — random-from-pool is optional. Reading can mix several skills. Math boards show **answers**; calls are the **problems**. Always a one-page checklist **calling sheet** (she marks it in a sleeve with dry erase); optional **cut-apart calling cards, 12 per page, thick dotted lines**. Title "Bingo" big Fredoka Bold; skill bubble top right.
- **Mystery Picture** (portrait): pick a pixel image (thumbnails by theme). Colors listed **most pixels → fewest**; each color has an editable swatch (recolors all its pixels), editable crayon name, and a skill (reading) or answer number (math). **Pixel editor** paints single squares with undo/reset. Key = **crayon shapes with the color name inside**; label shows **just the skill** (no "words") or just the number ("10 ="). Colored answer key. The puzzle never says what the picture is.
  - **"Don't color the background"** (per picture, ON by default): background gets no crayon/skill; its squares get words/problems that match **none** of the key; directions say to leave unmatched squares uncolored; answer key leaves them white. OFF = old "White" crayon.
  - Background words must be **decodable**: only from regular skills placed **at or before the latest assigned skill/unit/category**, excluding words from assigned lists, words containing assigned spellings, and assigned short vowels. If the pool is empty, show a clear UI warning (never use later words); small pools repeat spread out. Math background = problems whose answers aren't any color's number.
- **Read and Draw** (portrait, `readdraw.js`): 6 decodable sentences per page in a 2x3 grid, a numbered circle and a rounded drawing box under each sentence; Name line at the very top, skill bubble top right. **Each sentence sits right on top of its drawing box** (the text block is bottom-aligned in a row, so a short sentence is not left floating above a gap); a row of short sentences gets taller drawing boxes. The sentence text item uses `exact:true` in `core.js`, which writes an exact line spacing (size x 1.25) into the PPTX so PowerPoint and Slides match the preview; leave other text on the default spacing. Pages option 1-10 (default 2). Pulls by Unit / Through Unit (all sentences from those units) or by any other skill (sentences containing that skill's words, up to its unit). Skills with fewer than 6 sentences are hidden for this product.
- **Word-work pages** (portrait, `wordwork.js`; data from `site/data/wordinfo.js`, built by `spec/build_wordinfo.py`; **no answer keys**). Six products: **Read and Draw: Words** (the word above a rounded drawing box; 9, 10 or 12 boxes per page, default 12), **Count the Sounds** (word, five joined sound boxes, a count square, writing lines; 10, 11 or 12 rows, default 12), **Read, Draw and Count** (6 + 6 on one page), **Letter Shapes**, **Missing Letters** (4, 6 or 8 words per page, default 6), and **Letter Shapes + Missing Letters** (4 + 4 on one page). These three are not called "tricky word" pages because she can type any word: default skill label "Word Work" (editable), default page titles "Letter Shapes", "Missing Letters" and "Letter Shapes and Missing Letters", and a **Page title** box on the site (cleared = no title; the content then starts 0.45in higher so the boxes get more room). Internal ids stay `wwshapes`, `wwmissing`, `wwtricky`. The bank's tricky words are still the clickable word buttons. The phonics products pull from the chosen skill's decodable words (Read and Draw: drawable words; Count the Sounds: words with 2 to 5 sounds, `-ing` words not used, qu and x count as one sound); the tricky products have no skill picker: Samantha clicks words (the bank's tricky words) or types her own (letters only), and they go on pages in the order given. If a pool is smaller than the chosen page size the page uses the largest size it can fill (9/10/12 or 10/11/12) and says so; skills with too few words are hidden. Student pages use black, white and gray only (gray writing lines and letter-shape boxes, all one weight). The Name line and skill pill sit 0.17in lower than they used to so they clear the page border; Word Sort, Word Maze, Picture Write and Read and Draw were moved the same way (their content starts a little lower). Cold Read, Mystery Picture, Bingo and the landscape products have no border near the header and were left alone. On these pages the letter-shape boxes and the word with blanks are centered in their half, with the writing lines to the right. **The two-activity page has no section labels** and a smaller word bank (16pt); its Letter Shapes rows keep full-size boxes (0.32in per row, so tall letters 0.64in) and the Missing Letters rows take the remaining height (never under 0.66in per row). **She can choose which letters are blanked** on Missing Letters (and the pair page): under the word box a "Missing letters" list shows each word as letter tiles; clicking a tile hides it (black) or shows it again, a Reset button returns one word to automatic, "Reset all letters" clears every choice, and showing every letter goes back to automatic too. Her choices are kept in `twBlanks` in `app.js`, cleaned by `T.tricky.cleanBlanks`, and passed to `mlPatterns` as `custom`; her letters are never overridden (she can leave three or more letters showing), and if a chosen pattern would also fit another word on the page, the other word moves to a later page. Typing stays wide open (any letters-only word). Rules carried over from the Daily Literacy Practice pages: tall letters (t d f h k l b and capitals) fill two rows, letters that hang (q y p g j) the lower two, the rest the middle row; **words on one Letter Shapes page never share a shape**; Missing Letters keeps **only two letters visible** (one in a two-letter word), prefers blanking the irregular letters (`data-src/tricky_irregular.json`), and **no word's blanks may also fit another word on the page**; the word bank is shuffled so no word stays in its own row. Words are packed onto pages in her order, moving a word to a later page when it would break those rules. Tests: `node tests/wordwork.js`.
- **Bundles** (`js/bundle.js`): a bundle downloads as a **ZIP of separate files** (changed Nov 10: separate files so products can be different shapes, landscape + portrait mixed). The zip holds **`01 Cover and Contents`** (bundle cover with the typed title and skills, plus a What's Included page listing the numbered files) and then one numbered file per set, e.g. **`02 Roll and Read - sh`**, **`03 Read and Draw - Unit 3`**. Every file is included as PPTX and PDF. Each set file is exactly the standalone product (its own cover, directions page, pages and Terms of Use) in its own orientation. No divider pages and no orientation warning. Any product types can be mixed. Bundle title is typed in (auto-suggested until she types). Do not go back to one combined file.
- **TpT listing images** (`js/listing.js`, button "TpT listing images" for single products and bundles): zip of 2000x2000 PNGs: colorful cover (bold title, skill pill, grade callout, tilted/stacked page mockups, "answer key" burst if one exists, store name, logo), one preview per picked page, a "What's Included" image, and `listing.txt`. Pages come from the **page picker** (click pages in the preview strip; the first two or three go on the cover; none picked = automatic choice, the first student page of each set in bundles; the listing zip is a separate download from the bundle zip). Grade text and one of five color themes (apple = brand red, sky, sunny, grape, mint) are options; the grade is a listing-image callout only, never printed in product files. The listing images are marketing art, so they use color and Fredoka Bold callouts; the product files stay black and white.
- **Listing Image Maker** (`site/listing.html`, linked from the header): drop in any PDF / PPTX / pictures, pick pages, edit every text on the listing images, live preview, zip download. Reuses `LISTING.build` from `listing.js` (`spec.text` overrides). Details, PPTX renderer limits and tests: see `LISTING_TOOL.md`. The listing images' store-name pill is small and level with the logo, and the yellow bottom banner must stay perfectly level (no tilt).



## Testing checklist
Serve `site/` (`cd site && python3 -m http.server 8812`), run `node tests/suite.js` (data build, sentence checker, every product x every skill, Read and Draw, mixed landscape + portrait bundle zip, picker, listing zips, PPTX XML rules) `node tests/wordwork.js` (word-work pages), `node tests/dropin.js` (Listing Image Maker), and `node tests/all.js` (every product to PDF/PPTX/PNG, output in /tmp/tpt-test-out). Also download PPTX + PDF for each product type, a bundle, and the pixel editor; render PDFs to PNG and check visually; optionally convert PPTX with LibreOffice; grep the PPTX XML for `schemeClr`/`tableStyleId` (must be absent). Then zip `site/` contents. **Ask Samantha before publishing.**
